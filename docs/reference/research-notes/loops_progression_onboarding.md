# Core Loops, Progression and Onboarding in Top Roblox Games (2025-2026)

Source-quality note: Roblox Creator Docs and the GameAnalytics 2025 benchmark report are the strongest sources here. Wikipedia, Newzoo (via GameDevReports), PC Gamer, esports.gg and Destructoid are fine for facts and records. Game-guide sites (playnews.gg, holdtoreset, itemlevel, allthings.how, progameguides) are fan/SEO guides: usable for mechanics, but numbers may be out of date. RoWatcher.com articles (2026) read like opinion pieces, cite few primary sources, and contain at least one doubtful claim (a PS99 "rift" prestige system I could not confirm). Treat RoWatcher as low-confidence.

## Which games are "top" in 2025-2026 (context)

### Takeaway
2025 was led by a few huge idle and collect titles: Grow a Garden, Steal a Brainrot and 99 Nights in the Forest. Evergreen titles (Brookhaven, Blox Fruits, RIVALS, The Strongest Battlegrounds) stayed in the top 10 almost every month. Attention also became very concentrated in a few games.

### Cited Findings
- Steal a Brainrot drew 26.4B visits in Q3 2025, the highest figure cited. Grow a Garden drew 12.3B visits in Q2 2025 but had left the top 10 by December. 99 Nights in the Forest passed 10B visits within a few months of launch. Brookhaven RP and Blox Fruits were in the top 10 every month of 2025, and RIVALS and The Strongest Battlegrounds in 11 of 12 months — [Newzoo via GameDevReports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/newzoo-top-roblox-games-in-2025)
- Q2 2025 was the first quarter in which a game passed 10M peak CCU. The top title's share of top-10 visits rose from 22% (Q1) to 43% (Q4), and by year end 3 games held over 70% of top-10 visits. Platform DAU averaged 126.5M (+53% YoY), with 123.8B hours (+69%) — [Newzoo via GameDevReports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/newzoo-top-roblox-games-in-2025)
- Grow a Garden (dev: Jandel) was created in March 2025 and hit 5M+ CCU on May 18, 2025, beating Blox Fruits' record — [esports.gg](https://esports.gg/news/roblox/grow-a-garden-breaks-blox-fruits-record-for-most-concurrent-users-on-roblox/). It later reached about 22.3M CCU — [Wikipedia: Steal a Brainrot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot)
- Steal a Brainrot (SpyderSammy / DoBig Studios) launched May 16, 2025. CCU went from 5M (Jul 2025) to 20M (Aug 2025) to 24M (Sep 13, 2025, "Extinct Event") to 25.4M (Oct 2025, the first game past 25M). Roblox set a platform record of 47.4M CCU on Aug 23, 2025 during a mock "admin war" between the two games. Both games rely on developer-hosted "admin abuse" live events that hand out exclusive items — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot)

### Inferences
- The biggest 2025 hits pair a simple, readable loop with passive income and scheduled live events. A golem idle game fits that pattern well.

### Gaps
- I found no authoritative per-genre share of playtime for 2025-2026.

## Core loop patterns by genre (30-second / 5-minute / session)

### Takeaway
Every top genre uses the same skeleton: a fast action that pays (click, cast, buy, plant, kill), a cash-out step (sell or collect), reinvestment in a multiplier (upgrade, rod, seed, pet), and a gate that the multiplier opens (new zone, island, sea, rebirth). Timers (restocks, weather, nights, payouts) set the rhythm of the 5-minute loop.

### Cited Findings
**Farming/idle: Grow a Garden**
- The loop is buy seeds, plant, harvest, sell, and reinvest in more seeds until the plot is full. Crops keep growing and fruiting while the player is offline — [esports.gg](https://esports.gg/news/roblox/grow-a-garden-breaks-blox-fruits-record-for-most-concurrent-users-on-roblox/)
- The Seed Shop restocks every 5 minutes, and the stock is global (rejoining does not reset it). A 79 Robux purchase force-refreshes the stock. Seed rarities run Common, Uncommon, Rare, Legendary, Mythical, plus secret seeds. Top seeds sell out within seconds of a restock — [itemlevel.net](https://itemlevel.net/complete-beginners-guide-for-grow-a-garden-roblox/)
- Multi-harvest plants (Strawberry, Tomato, Corn) regrow after harvest and are recommended early for passive income — [itemlevel.net](https://itemlevel.net/complete-beginners-guide-for-grow-a-garden-roblox/)
- Weather adds value multipliers and mutations. Rain gives +50% growth and the Wet mutation, Storm gives the Storm mutation, and Sunny gives about +10% growth. Gold and Rainbow mutations are rare. Fruit weight scales sale value. Selling while playing with friends gives +10% cash. Daily quests come from an NPC. Gear price examples: Basic Sprinkler $20,000, Watering Can $50,000 — [itemlevel.net](https://itemlevel.net/complete-beginners-guide-for-grow-a-garden-roblox/)
- A loop reading based on those mechanics:
  - 30 seconds: harvest, plant, check a crop's growth percentage.
  - 5 minutes: wait for the seed restock, sell, buy a rarer seed.
  - Session: chase mutations during weather events and expand the garden.

**Collect-and-steal tycoon: Steal a Brainrot**
- Each player gets a base with pedestals and a little starting cash. A central conveyor belt cycles brainrots of different rarities for sale. A placed brainrot earns income per second. Players steal from rival bases with a progress bar that partly resets when interrupted, and defend with locks and shields. A Fast Steal gamepass cuts theft time by 40% — [playnews.gg](https://www.playnews.gg/en/guides/steal-a-brainrot-roblox-the-complete-strategy-rebirth-and-tier-list-guide-for-july-2026); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot)
- Rarity income tiers (fan guide, July 2026) show about 10x steps per tier:

  | Tier | Income per second |
  |---|---|
  | Common | $1-5 |
  | Rare | $25-100 |
  | Epic | $250-1,000 |
  | Legendary | $3,000-10,000 |
  | Mythic | up to $50,000 |
  | Brainrot God | over $200,000 |

  — [playnews.gg](https://www.playnews.gg/en/guides/steal-a-brainrot-roblox-the-complete-strategy-rebirth-and-tier-list-guide-for-july-2026). The same guide gives a conflicting Mythic threshold elsewhere.
- Loop reading:
  - 30 seconds: buy off the conveyor and place.
  - 5 minutes: income ticks up, then raid or defend.
  - Session: build up to rebirth.

**Survival co-op: 99 Nights in the Forest**
- A countdown to night starts on load-in. Players feed the campfire (HP regenerates near it), chop and replant trees, loot chests, and upgrade the crafting bench in tiers (Tier 3 unlocks the crock pot and lightning rod). Threats escalate across nights: the Owl appears after night 5 and Cultist invasions grow. A Pelt Trader visits from day 2 — [PC Gamer](https://pcgamer.com/games/roblox/99-nights-in-the-forest-tips/)
- Meta progression: diamonds (from achievements and codes) buy classes, for example Scavenger for 25 diamonds, and talent rerolls. These persist between runs — [PC Gamer](https://pcgamer.com/games/roblox/99-nights-in-the-forest-tips/)
- Loop reading:
  - 30 seconds: gather wood.
  - 5 minutes: one day/night cycle.
  - Session: one run toward 99 nights, plus diamonds for permanent unlocks.

**Fishing: Fisch**
- The loop is cast (release timing sets distance), shake (each click cuts bite wait by about 0.5 s), reel minigame (keep the indicator in the bar), then sell to a merchant. A Perfect Catch gives C$10 and 1.5 EXP — [Destructoid](https://www.destructoid.com/fisch-roblox-beginners-guide/)
- The economy ladder:
  - Rods: Carbon Rod C$2,000 (early), Rod of the Depths about C$750,000.
  - Boats: Rowboat C$700, Hovercraft C$7,000, Jetski C$50,000 (needs level 50).
  - Gates: a quest at level 200 unlocks the Second Sea, where rods are nerfed and First Sea currency doesn't work. Over 12 named islands, starting at Moosewood. A bestiary is the collection book (key 3).
  
  — [Destructoid](https://www.destructoid.com/fisch-roblox-beginners-guide/)

**RPG grinder: Blox Fruits**
- Zones are gated by level: Second Sea at level 700 plus a quest chain (Ice Admiral boss), Third Sea at level 1,500 plus giving up a fruit worth at least $1M and a boss chain — [Pro Game Guides](https://progameguides.com/roblox/all-sea-requirements-in-blox-fruits-explained-roblox/)

**Tower defense / anime unit collectors (Anime Expeditions as a proxy)**
- An AFK Portal auto-replays a stage you've already cleared. It pays coins, gems and items every interval equal to your fastest clear time (for example every 3 minutes). Idling in the normal lobby disconnects you after about 5 minutes. A VIP pass (320 Robux) speeds AFK payouts by 25% — [allthings.how](https://allthings.how/anime-expeditions-afk-mode-in-roblox-how-the-afk-world-works/)

**Simulators (PS99, Bee Swarm, AFS X)**
- PS99: pets auto-collect, and gem and damage numbers reach billions and quadrillions. Bee Swarm rewards leaving the client running in-game rather than true offline progress. AFS X has AFK training zones with a stats-per-minute UI — [RoWatcher (low confidence)](https://rowatcher.com/news/the-afk-revolution-how-idle-mechanics-conquered-roblox)

### Inferences
- For Grow a Golem:
  - 30-second loop: the golem kills or mines a node and drops minerals.
  - 5-minute loop: return to the forge, craft a part, and notice a visible power jump. A global 5-minute shop or ore restock timer would echo Grow a Garden.
  - Session loop: unlock the next floating island, then rebirth.
- About 10x income steps per rarity tier (as in Steal a Brainrot) are a readable default for artifact and mineral tiers.

### Gaps
- I found no verified sources in this pass for Tower Defense Simulator, Anime Defenders, Dead Rails, Bee Swarm or classic tycoon specifics (wave counts, unit rarity odds, honey/pollen numbers, dropper rates). I left these out rather than fill them from memory.
- No source gives measured 30-second, 5-minute or session durations. The loop readings above are my interpretation of the mechanics.

## Progression: rebirth, zone gating, rarity, collection, number scaling

### Takeaway
Rebirth/prestige is placed at zone milestones and grants a permanent multiplier and new features (not only bigger numbers). Zones are gated by level, currency or keys. Rarity ladders step up by about 10x, and collection books (index, bestiary) give a completionist goal.

### Cited Findings
- PS99 rebirths sit at fixed area milestones: Rebirth 1 at Area 25, Rebirth 2 at 50, 3 at 75, 4 at 99 (unlocks World 2), up to Rebirth 8-9 around Areas 199-219. Each of Rebirths 1-8 gives +75% pet strength. Each rebirth also unlocks a system:
  - R1: teleport and trading plaza
  - R2: clans, auto-hatch
  - R3: pet teams, Index rewards
  - R4: Ultimates, Mastery
  - R5: secret pets
  - R6: golden and charged eggs
  
  Diamond rewards run 10k to 25k to 50k to 100k to 500k. The guide's rebirth count is inconsistent (7 vs 9) — [Hold To Reset](https://holdtoreset.com/pet-simulator-99-rebirth-system-explained/)
- Steal a Brainrot rebirth wipes your brainrots. It grants a permanent earnings multiplier and tokens for more pedestals (up to 40), skins, passive abilities and VIP zones. From rebirth 3, Golden variants earn 4x and Rainbow variants earn 6x. The guide suggests a first rebirth after about 8-10 hours at about $100k/s income — [playnews.gg](https://www.playnews.gg/en/guides/steal-a-brainrot-roblox-the-complete-strategy-rebirth-and-tier-list-guide-for-july-2026)
- Zone gates by level: Blox Fruits at 700 and 1,500 — [Pro Game Guides](https://progameguides.com/roblox/all-sea-requirements-in-blox-fruits-explained-roblox/). Fisch at level 50 for the Jetski and level 200 for the Second Sea — [Destructoid](https://www.destructoid.com/fisch-roblox-beginners-guide/)
- Roblox advises keeping early XP thresholds low so players level fast, tuning thresholds live with Configs, and tracking how many users reach each progression level to fix steps that are too fast or too slow — [Roblox Docs: Onboarding](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/onboarding.md); [Roblox Docs: Retention](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/production/analytics/retention.md)
- Number-inflation risk: PS99 has had several gem value corrections and economy resets — [RoWatcher (low confidence)](https://rowatcher.com/news/the-afk-revolution-how-idle-mechanics-conquered-roblox)

### Inferences
- A model for golem rebirths: about +50-100% permanent power per rebirth, with rebirths at fixed island milestones. Each rebirth should unlock a new system (auto-forge, artifact slots, trading), as PS99 does. This keeps rebirth from feeling like pure loss for 9-15 year olds.
- K/M/B/T abbreviations are implied by these incomes (Brainrot God at over $200k/s, PS99 in the billions), but I found no source documenting the formatting convention itself.

### Gaps
- No verified XP-curve formulas from top games. No published session-length targets per genre from Roblox itself.

## Idle/AFK features

### Takeaway
Offline growth (Grow a Garden), AFK portals with timed payouts (anime TD games) and paid speed-ups to idle rates are now standard. Paid passes speed up idle gains rather than gate content.

### Cited Findings
- Grow a Garden crops grow while you're offline — [esports.gg](https://esports.gg/news/roblox/grow-a-garden-breaks-blox-fruits-record-for-most-concurrent-users-on-roblox/)
- Server and global timers: the Grow a Garden seed shop restocks every 5 minutes globally — [itemlevel.net](https://itemlevel.net/complete-beginners-guide-for-grow-a-garden-roblox/). The 99 Nights countdown to night starts on load — [PC Gamer](https://pcgamer.com/games/roblox/99-nights-in-the-forest-tips/)
- AFK portal: payouts arrive every best-clear-time interval, VIP gives +25%, and normal lobbies idle-kick at about 5 minutes — [allthings.how](https://allthings.how/anime-expeditions-afk-mode-in-roblox-how-the-afk-world-works/)
- Claimed design rules (low confidence):
  - Set offline gains close to, but below, active rates.
  - Show resource-per-hour readouts.
  - Make a 5-minute check-in satisfying.
  - Rely on update-driven return events rather than stamina systems.
  - Make Robux purchases compress time rather than gate content.
  - The article says PS99 offline collection runs at about 50% efficiency, capped by inventory.
  
  — [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/the-afk-revolution-how-idle-mechanics-conquered-roblox)

### Inferences
- Golem: offline mining at about 50% of the active rate, capped (for example 8-12 hours), with a "welcome back" claim screen. An Auto-Forge or 2x AFK speed pass fits the norm.

### Gaps
- The PS99 50% offline figure is unverified by any primary source.

## Onboarding / FTUE and retention benchmarks

### Takeaway
Roblox's own guidance is to reach the fun within 5 minutes or less, keep tutorials short (tooltips over long tutorials), give low early XP thresholds and enough starter currency, show short, mid and long-term goals, and end the first loop with a deliberate "moment of joy." Median D1 retention is only about 4-11%, rising with session length. Top-percentile games reach about 18-32% D1.

### Cited Findings
- FTUE is the first few minutes and is measured by D1 retention and onboarding funnel goals. Roblox's guidance:
  - Teach controls and the core loop, both what to do and why.
  - Get to the fun quickly with low early XP thresholds and moderate starter items or currency, tested via Experiments and tuned via Configs.
  - Show short, mid and long-term goals in the world or UI (Jailbreak's season-pass popup is the example).
  - End onboarding with a designed moment of joy: rewards, animations, effects.
  - Their example A/B test compares a shorter dialogue vs a guided arrow.
  
  — [Roblox Docs: Onboarding](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/onboarding.md)
- "Get users to the fun ideally in 5 minutes or less." Also from Roblox:
  - For D1: use a short tutorial or tooltips, avoid long tutorials, create a satisfying moment after the first loop, show future progress, and test performance across devices.
  - For D7: add short and long-term goals and content variety, and balance progression speed.
  - Funnel method: list the core-loop steps, log each with funnel events, and fix the biggest drop-offs (for example a hard-to-find location).
  - Cadence: small updates every 2-4 weeks, big ones every 2-3 months.
  
  — [Roblox Docs: Retention](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/production/analytics/retention.md)
- An opinion piece (Aug 2026) argues the retention cliff is in the first 90 seconds. It recommends letting players act before reading text (Adopt Me! puts a baby in your hands at spawn) and using environmental cues (glowing doors, arrows, NPCs walking a direction) instead of text prompts — [RoWatcher (opinion, low confidence)](https://rowatcher.com/news/your-first-90-seconds-are-destroying-roblox-retention)
- GameAnalytics 2025 Roblox Benchmark Report (data Jan 2023 to Jul 2025), game-level retention by average session length. Values are median / 90th percentile / 98th percentile:

  | Session length | D1 | D7 | D30 |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 0-3 min | 4.31% / 9.25% / 12.33% | 0.41% / 1.78% / 3.16% | 0.13% / 0.62% / 0.81% |
  | 7-12 min | 7.91% / 11.55% / 15.81% | 0.90% / 2.04% / 3.57% | 0.24% / 0.75% / 1.49% |
  | 13-18 min | 10.24% / 14.96% / 20.85% | 1.20% / 2.70% / 4.57% | — |
  | 19-24 min | 11.46% / 18.20% / 32.30% | 1.61% / 5.15% / 8.54% | — |
  | 25+ min | 10.76% / 18.86% / 25.41% | 1.82% / 5.86% / 7.01% | 0.50% / 1.45% / 2.87% |

  — [GameAnalytics 2025 Roblox Benchmark Report (PDF)](https://investgame.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/2025-Roblox-Benchmark-Report.pdf)
- Other figures from the same report:
  - Platform-wide (GA-tracked games): median 2 sessions per user per day (90th percentile 6, 95th percentile 8).
  - Average session length: 25th percentile about 2 min in 2025 (down from 4 in 2023), 95th percentile about 37 min (up from 25).
  - Daily playtime: 95th percentile 93.8 min.
  - Games with 19+ minute sessions have median sessions per day near or above 2 and much higher ARPPU (top percentiles in the high $20s-$30s).
  - Device mix: PC+mobile is about half of players, console about 2%.
  
  — [GameAnalytics 2025 Roblox Benchmark Report (PDF)](https://investgame.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/2025-Roblox-Benchmark-Report.pdf)
- Claimed (unsourced) session lengths: idle/AFK games run 45-90+ min, competitive 15-25, social 30-45 — [RoWatcher (low confidence)](https://rowatcher.com/news/the-afk-revolution-how-idle-mechanics-conquered-roblox)
- Fisch has an in-game tutorial that a reviewer calls "pretty good" but incomplete — [Destructoid](https://www.destructoid.com/fisch-roblox-beginners-guide/)

### Inferences
- Targets for the golem game:
  - D1 above 11% (above the median for 19+ minute games), stretch goal 18% or more (90th percentile).
  - D7 above 1.6%, stretch 5% or more.
  - Average session 19+ minutes, which idle play makes easier.
- Suggested FTUE:
  - Spawn already riding the golem, which starts fighting within about 10 seconds.
  - Show the first mineral pickup within 30 seconds, with a Beam/arrow to the forge.
  - Complete the first forge within about 2 minutes, with a celebratory VFX "moment of joy."
  - Show the next island's cost on screen.
  - Run tooltips instead of a forced text tutorial.
  - Log every step as funnel events.
